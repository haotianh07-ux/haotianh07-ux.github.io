My Activities & Honors

EXTRACURRICULAR / PREP

miRcore Computational Biology & Biotech Sequencing Camp

I spent two weeks in San Diego attending camps hosted by MiRcore, one of the biggest biology research centers, and the University of Michigan. The first week, I attended the Computational Biology Camp. We learned a relatively basic introductory level of Biology and went to visit the MiRcore research lab. We also learned to use research tools like Kegg, String, and NHGRI. On the last day, we made a presentation on Hemophilia and the genomes that could have contributed to it. The second camp was a sequencing camp. The focus was more on coding and biotechnology. We learned to use the Great Lakes System, a supercomputer system created by the University of Michigan. Using the system, we could decode and analyze genomic sequences, allowing us to do deep research. The last day, we had a presentation on the idea of using Antibodies for COVID-19.

40 hrs/wk | 2 wks/yr | Grade: 9
EXTRACURRICULAR / PREP

Global Youth Leadership Programme UN-SDGs Bootcamp & Forum

I attended the UN-SDGs Bootcamp and Forum in Okinawa. During the forum, we had discussions and lectures on topics regarding SDGs. Also, my classmates and I presented our project during the forum. We designed an AI trash bin that would identify the trash thrown into the trash can and make simple classifications. We came up with this idea because we discovered that many people, even though they do know that recyclable trash should be categorized, do not know which category it should be in. The main event of the forum was to draft the 2024 Bangkok Youth Sustainability Declaration. We were divided into groups and discussed each aspect of the SDGs sustainability goals, drafting the whole declaration together.

16 hrs/wk | 1 wk/yr | Grade: 10
